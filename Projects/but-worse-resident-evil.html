But Worse: 3D Resident Evil

A 3D game project inspired by Resident Evil, exploring 3D environments, atmosphere and interactive gameplay.

Unity C# 3D Game Design
01 Overview

Exploring survival horror in 3D.

But Worse: 3D Resident Evil is a 3D game project inspired by the Resident Evil series and its distinctive survival horror style.

The project focuses on creating a 3D environment where exploration, atmosphere and interaction come together to create a horror-inspired gameplay experience.

The project was an opportunity to experiment with 3D game development and translate the visual and atmospheric qualities of survival horror into an interactive experience.
