08 Physical prototype · Interaction

Dance Pads

A physical interactive project exploring movement, playful interaction and human-computer interaction through custom dance pads.

HCI Prototype Interaction Physical Computing
01 Overview

Making interaction physical.

Dance Pads is a physical interaction project exploring how movement can become part of the interaction between a person and a digital system.

The project uses custom-built dance pads as an alternative input method, allowing the player to interact with the experience through physical movement.

The focus was on creating an interaction that is playful, engaging and different from traditional keyboard or controller input.

02 My role

Designing interaction through movement.

I worked on the design and development of the physical interaction, focusing on how movement could be translated into meaningful input.

The project required considering both the physical design of the prototype and the interaction between the user and the system.
