Blue Planet

An adaptive interactive experience exploring how player choices can shape a virtual planet and influence its ability to support life.

Unity Adaptive Systems Game Design Learning
01 Overview

Exploring life through an adaptive world.

Blue Planet is an interactive learning experience designed around the idea of adaptation and the conditions needed for life to exist.

The player controls different aspects of a virtual planet and makes decisions about its environment, such as its atmosphere and distance from the sun.

The experience responds to the player's choices, creating different outcomes and encouraging exploration of how environmental conditions affect life.

02 My role

Designing an experience that adapts to the player.

I worked on the design and development of the interactive experience, focusing on how player decisions could influence the virtual world.

The project required connecting player choices with changes in the environment and creating meaningful outcomes from those decisions.
