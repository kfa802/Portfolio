04 VR · Escape room

Harry Potter Escape Room in VR

An immersive VR escape room inspired by the magical world of Harry Potter, combining exploration, puzzles and interactive challenges.

Unity C# VR OpenXR
01 Overview

Exploring magic through virtual reality.

Harry Potter Escape Room is a VR experience built around exploration, puzzle solving and interaction in a magical environment.

The player explores the environment, discovers clues and interacts with objects to progress through the escape room.

The project focuses on creating an immersive experience where the environment itself becomes part of the gameplay.

Designing interaction in VR.
