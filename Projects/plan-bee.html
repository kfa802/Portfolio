01 Game development · Unity

Plan Bee

A 2D top-down strategy game about managing a bee colony through changing seasons, resources and environmental threats.

Unity C# Game Design Systems
01 Overview

Managing a colony through an unpredictable environment.

Plan Bee is a 2D top-down strategy game where the player manages a growing bee colony and its resources.

The game revolves around balancing the needs of the hive while dealing with changing seasons, weather and other environmental challenges.

The project was developed in Unity and focuses on creating interconnected gameplay systems that make the colony feel like a living system.

02 My role

Gameplay systems & programming

My main role in the project was working with the bee systems, gameplay logic and several of the environmental systems.

I worked with the base Bee class and inheritance structure used by the different types of bees, as well as systems for placing and managing different hive zones.

I also worked on the seasonal and weather systems, including how environmental conditions affect the bees and their ability to collect resources.

03 Systems

Building systems that work together.

A large part of the project was making separate gameplay systems communicate with each other without making the project difficult to manage.

01

Bee system

Different bee types inherit from a shared base class while having their own behaviours and roles.

02

Hive zones

Zones control where bees can perform specific activities and manage the available capacity within the hive.

03

Seasons

Spring, summer, autumn and winter change the conditions the colony has to deal with.

04

Weather

Weather conditions can affect bee behaviour and resource gathering, creating additional challenges for the player.

04 Prototyping

Testing ideas before building the final systems.

Prototyping was an important part of the development process. We used simple prototypes to test core gameplay ideas and explore how different systems could work together.

This allowed us to experiment with mechanics, identify problems early and gradually develop the systems into the final game.
