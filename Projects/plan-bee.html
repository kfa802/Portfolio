01 Game development · Unity

Plan Bee

A 2D top-down strategy game about managing a bee colony through changing seasons, resources and environmental threats.

Unity C# Game Design Systems Prototyping
01 Overview

Managing a bee colony through changing seasons.

Plan Bee is a 2D top-down strategy game where the player manages a growing bee colony and its resources.

The player must manage limited resources to buy new bee types with different functions and expand the hive with different zones to protect the colony and queen from threats like snow and wasps.

The project was developed in Unity and focuses on creating interconnected gameplay systems that make the colony feel like a living system.

02 My role

Gameplay systems & programming

My main role in the project was working with the bee systems, gameplay logic and environmental systems.

I worked on the base classes and inheritance structure for the different types of bees and enemies. I also developed systems for managing hive zones and expanding the hive through a purchase system.

I also worked on the seasonal and weather systems and how they affect the bees and their ability to collect resources. For example, during winter, forager bees cannot collect pollen, which means the player has to plan ahead and manage their resources carefully. This is important since pollen is turned into honey by the worker bees, which is then used as currency to expand the hive and buy new bees.

03 Systems

Building systems that work together.

A large part of the project was making the different gameplay systems work together while keeping the code manageable. The bee, hive, resource, season and weather systems all affect each other, so changes in one system can also affect the others.

01

Bee Systems

Different bee types inherit from a shared base class while having their own behaviours and roles in the hive.

02

Hive zones

Zones control where bees can perform specific activities. Buying new zones also increases the hive's capacity.

03

Seasons & Weather

Each season changes the conditions the colony has to deal with, while weather can affect bee behaviour and enemy activity.

04

Purchase System

Forager bees collect pollen, which worker bees turn into honey. The player can then use the honey as currency to buy new bees and zones.

04 Prototyping

Testing ideas before building the final systems.

Prototyping was an important part of the development process. We used simple prototypes to test core gameplay ideas and explore how different systems could work together.

This allowed us to experiment with mechanics, identify problems early and gradually develop the systems into the final game.
