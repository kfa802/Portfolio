03 VR experience · Unity

Hearing Loss Experience

An immersive VR experience designed to communicate what everyday situations can feel like with hearing loss.

Unity C# VR OpenXR
01 Overview

Experiencing communication from another perspective.

This project is a VR experience focused on creating empathy around hearing loss.

Instead of explaining hearing loss only through information, the experience lets the user interact with an environment where understanding conversations and sounds becomes more difficult.

The project was developed in Unity with virtual reality as the main platform.

02 My role

VR interaction & experience design.

I worked on the Unity implementation of the VR experience and the systems needed to make the interaction feel natural inside the virtual environment.

The project uses Unity's XR tools and OpenXR to create an experience designed for a VR headset.
