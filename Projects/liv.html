08 Serious game · Unity

LIV

A serious game exploring how interactive experiences can support knowledge and awareness around mental health and suicide prevention among students in higher education.

Unity C# Serious Game HCI Research Interaction Design
01 Overview

Using games to support awareness.

LIV is a serious game developed to explore how interactive media can be used to promote knowledge and awareness around suicide prevention among students in higher education.

The project combines game design, interaction, narrative and educational content to create an experience that encourages players to engage with the subject in an interactive format.

02 Research

A serious game as a learning approach.

The project was developed as part of a research project investigating whether a serious game could help promote knowledge about suicide prevention among students in higher education.

The project resulted in the research paper “A Serious Game for Promoting Knowledge about Suicidal Thoughts for Students at Higher Education” , published at GoodIT '24.

03 My role

Developing the interactive experience.

I contributed to the development of the serious game, working with Unity and C# to build the interactive experience and implement the game's systems.

My work focused on translating the project's educational goals into interactive gameplay, while considering usability, interaction and how information was presented to the player.

04 Development

Designing for a sensitive subject.

Because the project deals with a sensitive subject, the design focused on presenting information in a respectful and accessible way.

The game combines visual communication, interaction and educational content to create an experience that is approachable while still communicating the seriousness of the topic.
