02 Learning game · Unity

A Learning Game about Women's Suffrage in Denmark

An educational learning game exploring student engagement and learning outcomes compared to a digital text-based approach.

Unity C# Learning Games Bachelor Project Collaboration Arbejdermuseet A/B Testing Narrative
01 Overview

Learning history through interaction.

The project is a narrative-driven learning game about the fight for women's suffrage in Denmark from 1896 to 1918. Designed for students in 7th–9th grade, the player follows Agnes through a third-person perspective and experiences important historical events and years through her story, choices, and interactions.

The game combines narrative, gameplay, and minigames that challenge players on the historical knowledge they have acquired throughout the experience.

02 Findings

Evaluation and Findings.

To evaluate the learning experience, we conducted A/B testing comparing the game-based approach with traditional text-based learning. We measured both player engagement and learning outcomes to compare the students' experience and learning across the two approaches.

While there was no statistically significant difference in learning outcome, the game showed a medium effect size, suggesting a potential positive effect with a larger sample.

Even though the learning outcome was inconclusive, the game group was significantly more engaged, with higher scores in focused attention, perceived usability, and reward. Overall, the results suggest that while the game did not significantly improve short-term learning outcomes, it successfully increased student engagement.

03 My role

Designing interaction and gameplay.

Although our bachelor project required each member to take part in all aspects of the project, including testing, design, and implementation, my primary focus was on the development of the game in Unity.

I developed several of the core systems the game is built around, including the game events that control the order of events and ensure that certain events can only be accessed after completing previous ones. I also designed and implemented the player interactions, animations, movement, and behaviour.

Another part of my role was shaping the visual style and atmosphere of the game. I adjusted the environmental settings and added rain and other particle effects to make the environment feel more realistic and alive.

04 Collaborations

Collaborations and Historical Content.

The project was developed in collaboration with Lasse Madsen at Højdevangskolen, who provided access to our intended target group of 7th–9th grade students for testing and feedback. This allowed us to test the game with the students it was designed for and use their feedback to improve the experience.

We also collaborated with Arbejdermuseet, whose historical expertise and feedback helped us strengthen the accuracy and communication of the game's historical content. Their input helped us consider how historical events and information could be presented in a way that was both historically accurate and understandable for our target audience.
