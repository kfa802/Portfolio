A Serious Game about Women's Suffrage in Denmark

An educational serious game exploring the fight for women's suffrage in Denmark through interactive gameplay and historical events.

Unity C# Serious Game History
01 Overview

Learning history through interaction.

Women's Suffrage is a serious game designed to communicate the history of the women's suffrage movement in Denmark through an interactive experience.

The player follows Agnes through a historical period where political and social change gradually leads towards women's right to vote.

Rather than presenting the history only as text, the project uses gameplay, choices and interaction to make the subject more engaging.
