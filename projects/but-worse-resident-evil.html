05 2024 3D game · Unity

Resident Evil (But Worse)

A third-person 3D horror shooter inspired by the Resident Evil games, built in Unity to explore enemy AI, raycasting, player movement and interactive 3D environments.

Unity C# 3D AI Game Development
01 Overview

Exploring 3D game development.

This project was an exploration of 3D game development in Unity, inspired by the atmosphere and gameplay of the Resident Evil games. The player explores a horror-inspired open world while fighting zombies and gathering ammo.

I built the project from the ground up, combining player movement, shooting, enemy AI and health systems into a complete playable experience.

The project also gave me the opportunity to work with NavMesh AI, raycasting and finite state machines, while experimenting with 3D environments, lighting, particles and atmospheric effects.

02 Development

Building the gameplay systems.

The development focused on building and connecting the different systems needed to create a responsive 3D horror game, from player controls and combat to enemy behaviour and environmental atmosphere.

01

Enemy AI

Enemies use NavMesh-based pathfinding and different behaviour states for patrolling, chasing and attacking the player.

02

Raycasting

Raycasting was used for aiming, shooting and detecting objects in front of the player, connecting the camera direction to gameplay interactions.

03

Finite State Machines

Finite state machines were used to structure player movement and aiming behaviours, making different states easier to manage and transition between.

04

3D Gameplay

Shooting, health, ammunition and enemy interactions were combined with lighting, particles and environmental effects to build the final experience.
