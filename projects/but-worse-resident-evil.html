Resident Evil (But Worse) .

A third-person 3D horror shooter inspired by the Resident Evil games, built in Unity with enemy AI, shooting and a strong focus on world building.

Type
3D game · Unity
Tools
Unity · C#
Topics
3D · AI · World Building · Game Development
01

Exploring 3D game development

This project was an exploration of 3D game development in Unity, inspired by the atmosphere of the Resident Evil games. The player explores a horror-inspired environment, shooting zombies that attack when you get too close, while collecting ammo and health herbs along the way.

Zombies spawn throughout the map, and Mr. X constantly chases the player, so you have to keep moving to survive.

A big focus of the project was world building, using terrain tools, particle effects, lighting and audio to create the atmosphere.

02

Building the gameplay systems

The development focused on connecting the systems needed for a responsive 3D horror game, from player controls and shooting to enemy AI and environmental atmosphere.

01

Enemy AI

Zombies spawn across the map and attack when the player is within range, while Mr. X constantly chases the player using NavMesh pathfinding.

02

Raycasting

Raycasting was used for aiming, shooting and detecting objects in front of the player, connecting the camera direction to gameplay interactions.

03

Finite State Machines

Finite state machines were used to structure player movement and aiming behaviours, making different states easier to manage and transition between.

04

World building

Terrain tools, particle effects, lighting and audio build the atmosphere, while ammo and health herbs reward exploring the map.
