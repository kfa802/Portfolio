Harry Potter Escape Room

An immersive VR escape room inspired by the magical world of Harry Potter, combining exploration, puzzles and interactive challenges.

Unity C# VR OpenXR Immersive Technologies Meta Quest
01 Overview

A Magical Escape Room in virtual reality.

Harry Potter Escape Room is a mini-project exploring different VR techniques and interactions through puzzle solving, exploration, and magical gimmicks.

The player takes on the role of a student exploring a magical environment, discovering clues and interacting with objects to solve puzzles and progress through the escape room.

The project focuses on how VR interactions, environmental design, and playful mechanics can be combined to create an immersive and engaging experience.

02 My role

Designing interaction in VR.

I primarily worked on the interactive systems that control the progression of the escape room. I developed the puzzle sequence, ensuring that the puzzles are completed in the intended order as the player progresses through the experience.

I also worked on the primary VR interactions, designing how the player interacts with objects and the environment. In addition, I contributed to the design of the puzzles and how they connect to the overall progression of the escape room.

Another part of my role was creating an atmosphere that felt authentic to the Harry Potter universe through visual design, lighting, and particle systems. These elements helped make the environment feel more magical and immersive.
