Zombie Game.

A VR zombie survival game exploring multimodal interaction through voice input, visual feedback, sound and haptics.

Type
Advanced HCI · VR Game
Tools
Unity · C# · VR
Topics
OpenXR · Multimodal Interaction · Haptic Feedback
01

Designing a multimodal VR survival experience

This project is a VR survival game focused on multimodal interaction and immersive feedback. The player navigates through a zombie-filled environment using VR controllers and their own voice.

The game combines visual, auditory and haptic feedback to communicate danger, guide the player and respond to their actions. Players use their voice to check door codes: a green light marks the correct path, while the wrong door has a proximity bomb. This includes voice-reactive navigation, explosions, zombie attacks and health interactions.

The project was developed in Unity for Meta Quest 2/3 and explores how different feedback modalities can work together to create a stronger sense of immersion and presence.

02

Developing a multimodal VR experience

I developed this individual mini-project for the Advanced Human-Machine Interaction course, including the gameplay systems, VR interactions, enemy behaviour and multimodal feedback. It was built in Unity for Meta Quest 2/3 using C#. 3D models, sounds and animations are from Sketchfab, FreeSound and Mixamo.

A major focus was combining visual, auditory and haptic feedback so that the game could respond to the player's actions and communicate events within the environment.

The different feedback systems were implemented as part of the core gameplay rather than as separate effects. For example, microphone input controls the intensity of lights and sounds, while explosions and enemy attacks provide corresponding haptic feedback through the VR controllers.

01

Voice-reactive navigation

The player's microphone input controls the intensity of lights and sounds around door codes, helping identify the correct path.

02

Haptic feedback

Custom haptic effects for explosions and player damage, created in Meta Haptics Studio. The explosion haptics are generated from the sound's waveform.

03

Zombie behaviour

Zombies react to the player's proximity by switching between idle, walking and attacking states with matching animations and sounds.

04

Health & combat

Grenades can defeat zombies, while damage and healing trigger combinations of visual, auditory and haptic feedback.
