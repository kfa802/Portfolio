About Me

I'm Kristine, a Medialogy student interested in the space between technology, design and interactive experiences.

I enjoy creating things that people can interact with. My interests range from game development and websites to experimental interfaces and immersive experiences.

A lot of my work combines programming with visual design, interaction and storytelling. I like experimenting with ideas and figuring out how technology can be used to create interesting experiences.

Through my studies, I've worked with Unity, C#, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, UI/UX, game design, VR and interactive media.

I'm always interested in learning something new, trying out different tools and turning ideas into something people can actually experience.
